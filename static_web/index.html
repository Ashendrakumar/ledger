Everything you need to manage money

Built for monthly planning, not just a list of expenses.

  • Plan and actual, side by side

    Plan each month, log what you really spend, and see the difference in rupees and percent, in red or green.

  • Balance that carries forward

    Each month shows only its own entries. Only the balance carries over from earlier months.

  • Standard plan from your salary

    Tell it your income once. It suggests rent, food, travel, bills and savings for you, and you can adjust every line.

  • Loans and EMI tracking

    Reducing or flat interest, a full schedule, prepayments, and one tap to mark an EMI as paid.

  • Goals you can edit

    Set a target, add any amount at any time, edit it or remove it when plans change.

  • Category breakdown

    Tap any total to see planned and actual for every category, with trends over the last six months.

  • Private by default

    Lock the app with your fingerprint or face. Export your data to CSV whenever you like.

  • Sign in with email only

    A 6-digit code to your email. No password to remember.

Up and running in three steps

It takes about a minute.

  1. Sign in with your email

    Enter your email and the 6-digit code we send you.

  2. Enter your monthly income

    Ladger builds a standard plan for rent, food, travel, bills and savings. Change any line you like.

  3. Log as you spend

    Add entries with the + button and see planned against actual for the month.

How to install the APK

Ladger is installed from a file, so Android asks for permission once.

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android. If Chrome warns that the file may be harmful, choose Download anyway.

  2. Allow installs from this browser

    When asked, open Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your browser or file manager.

  3. Open the file and tap Install

    Open Ladger.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder, then tap Install and Open.

Which Android versions work?

Android 8.0 and newer. Phones and tablets.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Your entries are kept on your phone, and sync when you are online and signed in.

How do I update?

Download the new APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays.
